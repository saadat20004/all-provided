Good to know

Before you arrive.

Quick answers to what guests ask most. Anything else, just add a note to your booking request.

What are the bathrooms and showers like?
Each area has clean on site portable toilets, serviced weekly and looked after daily. Hot showers are at Alder Lake Park, about 13 minutes away (open 8 am to 9 pm, $1 per 12 minutes). We give you towels, shampoo, conditioner and soap to take along.
Will I have internet?
Yes. Starlink Wi-Fi is available and cell service is excellent here, with guests reporting strong signal on AT&T and T-Mobile.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, every site is pet friendly. Please keep pets close, as bunnies roam the property.
Can we have a campfire?
Campfires are allowed at every site except the Road Corner Tent Site. Each site has a smokeless fire pit and firewood is sold on site.
Will it be cold at night?
Every site has warm blankets and a propane heater. Propane is $10 on site, or bring your own small green canister.
Which sites are near the road?
The Road Corner Tent Site and the Simple but Cozy Tent for Two sit close to the main road, so you will hear passing cars. We provide earplugs. Light sleepers should pick Stargazers Corner or Family Gathering Grounds for the most privacy.
